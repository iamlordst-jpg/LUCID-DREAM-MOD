# Dream entity roster

Design target: **20 distinct dream entities**. The initial roster is intentionally a mixture of friendly guides, neutral oddities, and hostile nightmare encounters. These are original gameplay concepts inspired by common dream motifs, not claims that dream entities have a single universal taxonomy.

## Guides and companions
1. **The Lantern Keeper** — wandering guide who illuminates hidden paths.
2. **Mothlight Wisp** — tiny floating light that leads players to dream landmarks.
3. **The Doorwalker** — appears beside locked doors and hints at alternate routes.
4. **The Paper Cartographer** — trades maps of places the player has not visited.
5. **The Echo Child** — repeats fragments of recent player movement as a harmless illusion.
6. **Cloud Whale** — enormous distant sky creature; a rare peaceful spectacle.
7. **The Velvet Cat** — vanishes around corners and leaves temporary safe zones.
8. **The Star Shepherd** — relocates small constellations and unlocks sky events.

## Neutral dream oddities
9. **The Hallway That Walks** — a moving corridor entity that changes nearby architecture.
10. **The Mirror Double** — mimics the player, then chooses a different route.
11. **The Clockwork Sleeper** — trades time-themed relics and changes local day/night.
12. **The Floating Market** — a travelling cluster of stalls and merchants.
13. **The Rain Collector** — gathers rain in glass vessels and changes local weather.
14. **The Unfinished Giant** — a colossal silhouette visible beyond fog, not always reachable.

## Nightmare encounters
15. **The Redactor** — erases temporary dream structures if ignored.
16. **The Staircase Stalker** — follows from a distance and retreats when observed.
17. **The Hollow Host** — invites players into a room that rearranges itself.
18. **The Static Choir** — creates disorienting sound and visual pulses.
19. **The Black-Eyed Conductor** — directs phantom-like minions through dream events.
20. **The Last Alarm** — rare chase encounter that ends when the player reaches a safe dream anchor.

## Implementation notes
The initial power command layer can summon a few vanilla stand-ins for immediate testing. The named roster above is the content design target for custom registered entities, models, animation controllers, AI goals, loot, spawn rules, and sounds in a later content pass. GeckoLib is the recommended animation dependency for that stage.
